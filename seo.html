Connect to Search Engines

Follow these steps once your website has its permanent public domain. This page is a setup guide; it does not claim that Google or Bing are connected yet.

  1. Set your domain: On your computer run SITE_URL=https://your-domain.com python3 scripts/build-seo.py before deploying. Replace the example with the real website address. This creates sitemap.xml, updates robots.txt, and adds canonical URLs to public pages.
  2. Google Search Console: Open Google Search Console. Add a Domain property (DNS verification) or URL-prefix property (HTML meta-tag verification). Submit https://your-domain.com/sitemap.xml.
  3. Bing Webmaster Tools: Open Bing Webmaster Tools. Import your verified Google Search Console property or verify ownership and submit the same sitemap.
  4. Google Business Profile: Open Google Business Profile and make sure the website URL, phone, services, and business details match the website.
  5. Monitor: Check indexing and sitemap reports after deployment. Search engines decide when and whether to index pages; submission is not a ranking guarantee.

Security: The private admin page is marked noindex and is not linked in the public sitemap. No verification tokens should be invented.
